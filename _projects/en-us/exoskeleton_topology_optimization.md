## Overview

This project focused on structural design and ANSYS-based optimization of an exoskeleton thigh component, with emphasis on balancing mechanical performance and computation efficiency.

## Technical Work

- Established ANSYS workflow for topology optimization and finite element analysis.
- Optimized meshing strategy to improve convergence speed.
- Reduced computational resource usage while preserving structural evaluation quality.

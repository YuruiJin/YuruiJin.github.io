## Overview

This project developed an intelligent robot for automated infant monitoring in nursery and healthcare environments. As the **Modeling and Control Lead**, I was responsible for mechanical structure design and robotic arm enhancement.

The project achieved **First Prize in Jiangsu Province** and **Second Prize in the National Finals**.

## Overview

As a core member of the **XJTLU Gmaster Team**, I was responsible for the mechanical design of infantry and engineering robots competing in the **RoboMaster University Championship** (2021–2023). Our team won **National First Prize** at the 2022 National Finals.

My contributions focused on two key areas: suspension system design for infantry robots and robotic arm mechanism design for engineering robots.

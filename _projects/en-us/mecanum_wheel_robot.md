## Overview

This project delivered a reconfigurable Mecanum wheel mobile robot prototype (RMO-08) through integrated mechanical design, motion modeling, and embedded control.

## Technical Work

- Built full 3D mechanical model and engineering drawings in SolidWorks.
- Implemented inverse kinematics to map chassis motion commands into wheel speeds for omnidirectional motion.
- Developed Arduino-based PID wheel velocity controllers for closed-loop regulation.
- Performed MATLAB simulation and hardware validation for control performance.

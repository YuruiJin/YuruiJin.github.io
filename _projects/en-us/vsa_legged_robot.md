## Overview

This SURF project studied a VSA-based single-leg mechanism and legged robot design to improve dynamic performance and energy usage.

## Technical Contributions

- Optimized the position and direction of elastic components in the single-leg mechanism.
- Improved jumping performance through mechanism-level stiffness tuning.
- Evaluated energy efficiency benefits under repeated jumping tasks.
- Related patent: **CN202310216442A**.

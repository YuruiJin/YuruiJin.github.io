## Overview

This ongoing project develops a fully autonomous pipeline on the Pololu 3pi platform, integrating perception, localization, and closed-loop control for embodied task execution.

## Technical Scope

- Sensor fusion of encoder, magnetometer, and infrared measurements.
- Real-time waypoint navigation and task-oriented motion planning.
- Object detection and physical interaction for pushing/transporting targets.
- Robust control design under uncertainty and disturbances.
